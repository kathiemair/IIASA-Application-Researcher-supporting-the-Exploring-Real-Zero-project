In this repository, you can find the code used for the data analysis and visualization of the results of my bachelor's thesis on the effect of gender and education of ministers holding climate-related portfolios on climate policy stringency, using data from six EU countries from ~1990 to 2022. I performed a fixed-effects panel regression model and various robustness tests. I used a dataset from the research project DEPART (PI: Univ.-Prof. Laurenz Ennser-Jedenastik), which I supported as a research assistant. Since data from this project is not published, I can not share this data on GitHub. For environmental policy stringency, I worked with the OECD Environmental Policy Stringency Index and the Climate Policy category of the Germanwatch Climate Change Performance Index. This thesis received the early-career prize of the Austrian Political Science Association.
